**Mechanic used** [PP §4]: Queens rules. Board = baking tray, region = dough patch, piece = tart. Place one per row, column and patch, and no two may touch, even diagonally. A single tap cycles empty → ✕ → tart. Boards run from 5×5 to 9×9.

## Recommendation: **B · Crisp Pastry Flat**
Flat shapes with a 2 px plum-ink outline, solid-bordered dough patches, each with a quiet same-hue pattern, on a pewter serving tray against rose-striped tea-room wallpaper. It reads best at 9×9 (37 px cells), it is the cheapest to produce in Godot (vector-like, small textures, which helps the ≤20 MB goal [CGR §4]), and it is the clearest of the three for a 35–65 audience. Players in that group report vision decline and quit over tiny cells and mis-taps [PP §4, S14]. A and C are kept as alternatives. C's stitched ✕ and doily could appear later as chapter décor.

| Option | Look | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A · Watercolour Tea Room `mocks/mock_A_390x844.png` | Soft washes, paper grain, no outlines | The prettiest, but patch borders soften at 8×9+ and painted textures cost size |
| **B · Crisp Pastry Flat** `mocks/mock_B_390x844.png` | Flat fills, ink outlines, crisp patterns | **Recommended**: most legible, smallest, easiest to keep on-model |
| C · Vintage China & Stitch `mocks/mock_C_390x844.png` | Cream porcelain, gilt rims, running-stitch borders, chintz | Very adult and distinctive, but dashed borders are weaker at 9×9 |

## Palette (`palette.png`, hex values in `tools/ttcolor.py`)
**UI:** rosewater #F6E3DE (bg) · clotted cream #FFF8EC (panels) · earl-grey ink #43283A (text, borders, ✕) · raspberry jam #B0365A (primary) · sage leaf #55724E (secondary, ticks) · pewter tray #A8A09F · toasted crust #C9803A (stars, "baked" glow) · cherry alert #A61E2E (conflict) · honey chip #EDBB80 (star/coin chip with ink text, 7.5:1; added after the contrast gate showed cream text on crust was only 3.0:1).
**Contrast:** `tools/gen_palette.py` runs a role-based PASS/FAIL gate (0 fails) and writes `tools/out/palette.json` and `tools/out/art_palette.gd`. Ink on cream 12.4:1. Cream on raspberry 5.7:1. Cream on sage 5.1:1. Ink on the darkest patch 3.9:1 (meets the non-text 3:1 minimum).
**9 dough patches, each with its own pattern** (so a 9×9 board is covered, and identity never relies on colour alone): strawberry #FA93AC dots · butter #FDE87D diagonal stripes · pistachio #B8DFA6 gingham · lavender #D5BCF1 lattice · honey #DDB664 crumb · blueberry #8E9CC9 shortbread brick · cocoa #AE906C diamond · rhubarb #CB6F91 sprig · vanilla #E8E6D9 pinstripe. Patches are also separated by thick ink borders.
**Type (all SIL OFL):** DM Serif Display for headings, Lexend for UI and numbers, Dancing Script for recipe names only. None overlaps with SD's fonts (Fraunces, Atkinson).

## Required checks
- **Colourblind** (`colorblind_check.png`; Machado 2009 at severity 1.0, ΔE2000 over every patch pair; data in `tools/cvd_pairs.json`). The colours were optimised so that **every pair stays ≥10 ΔE00** under normal vision, deuteranopia, protanopia and tritanopia. The closest pairs, which are close but still separable and also carried by pattern, are: deutan butter/honey 10.3 and pistachio/vanilla 10.5; protan lavender/blueberry 10.3 and butter/pistachio 10.6; tritan strawberry/honey 10.2 and lavender/vanilla 10.2. Under deutan and protan the reds and pinks become greys of different lightness. Keep patterns at the current strength and never ship a pattern-free mode.
- **Legibility from 5×5 to 9×9** (`legibility_5x5_to_9x9.png`, true 1× size, board 334 px on a 390-wide phone). Cells go from 67 px down to 37 px, and the tart from 60 px down to 33 px. B stays clean at 9×9. The ✕ and the conflict badge get small at 9×9 (badge ≈12 px), so the conflict also needs the shake plus the hatch.
- **Small sizes** (`small_size_test.png`: berry, lattice and lemon tarts at 48, 32 and 24 px). All read at 48 and 32. At 24 px, which is below our 33 px floor, the lattice turns to mush. The **berry tart is darkest and weakest on rhubarb/cocoa patches**, so give it the crust ring plus ink outline (as in B), or use the lemon tart as the default piece.
- **Cell states** (`cell_states_strip.png`): empty · ✕ (thin ink, no fill) · tart · **conflict** (cherry ring + diagonal hatch + ! badge, never colour alone) · **solved** (toasted glow + sage tick). Board solved = the whole tray gets a golden "baked" tint [PP §4].

## Portal and legal constraints (applied to every mock)
1. **Cozy for adults, not kids** (CG rejects kid-targeted games [CGR §1.4]; audience is women 35+ [PP §1]). The palette is restrained and refined, with plum ink instead of black, and there are no faces, mascots or toy-like chunky shapes. Copy stays plainly about pastry (PEGI-12).
2. **No toaster-pastry trade dress** [TM]. Kellogg has pending POP-TARTS filings covering browser games (IC 41) and puzzles (IC 28). So: **no rectangular frosted tarts, no sprinkles** (the old cocoa "sprinkles" pattern was replaced by diamonds), and no "pop" naming, puns or styling. Every tart is **round and fluted**, as berry, lattice or lemon. The cake-stand décor uses round domes, not bars.
3. **Desktop landscape backdrop** [CGR §1.1]: CG requires landscape on desktop, and plain bars aren't good enough. The portrait 390×844 play column is **centred at full height** on a static tea-room backdrop. The backdrop has rose-stripe wallpaper, a cream wainscot, a lace-curtained window onto **inland** green hills and a village spire, a dresser with china plates, teapots and tea caddies, a counter with a cake stand and round tarts under a glass dome, and a gingham runner. It is slightly blurred and washed (about 1.6 px blur plus 27% cream) so the board stays the focus, and it holds no gameplay or text. It ships as one WebP: about 23 KB at 1920×1080, q80 (measured), scaled to cover. Mocks: `mocks/landscape_B_1920x1080.png` and `mocks/landscape_B_at_CG_800x450.png`. At 800×450 the 7×7 board is about 178 px (25 px cells) and still readable. At 9×9 on 800×450 the cells are about 20 px, which is too small. For 8×8 and 9×9 on desktop, use a landscape layout with the board at full height and the recipe card and tools in a side column (to be mocked next).

## How it differs from Sudoku Dash (`sd_side_by_side.png`, `art_direction_board.png`)
SD's palette (`sd_art/palette.json`) is sea #2F7FA6 and #6BB8C9, sky #BFE3EF, teal #2C6E73 buttons, navy #1E2B38/#2E4A5C ink, terracotta roofs, gold #F2B33D, wood #8A5A3B and linen #F4EBDC. Its motifs are a harbour, lighthouse, boats, dock, awnings and a **coffee** cup, painted outdoors with a building-restoration meta. tea-bakery is indoors in an inland tea room, with rose, raspberry and plum, a pewter tray, teapots and tea caddies, and a recipe-card meta. **There is no blue water, no teal or navy, no wood dock and no coffee.** The fonts are different too.

## Risks
- Pastels plus patterns can look busy. Keep pattern alpha at ≤60% of the patch colour, and review on a real phone at 9×9.
- Pink-heavy UI could read as "for girls/kids". Mitigate with plum ink, sage, pewter and serif headings, and test against the cover CTR [PP §5].
- 9 patches: honey and butter are the closest pair under deutan and for normal vision, so avoid placing them adjacent where the generator can choose.
- B is the most "generic flat" option, so the china and doily décor (from C) are what give it its signature.
- **Housekeeping:** the proposal PNGs live under `art/` in the Godot project. I added `art/.gdignore` so they are not imported or exported (shipped art belongs in `assets/art/`). Remove it if `art/` is meant for shipped assets.

**Provenance:** every image was drawn procedurally in-house (`tools/*.py`, Pillow and numpy). There are no outside images and no AI image generation. The fonts are SIL OFL. The SD `theme_preview.png` is studio-internal and is used only for comparison. Nothing was added to `docs/art/LICENSES.md` because nothing is shipped yet; add the 3 font rows when they ship. There is no studio logo in any image.

## Open issues (follow-ups, 2026-10-25)
- **Wide desktop layout for 8×8 and 9×9** (`mocks/desktop_wide_9x9_1920x1080.png`, `mocks/desktop_wide_9x9_800x450.png`): the board fills the full height on the left, and the logo placeholder, recipe card, rules and a 2×2 grid of buttons sit in a right-hand column. **9×9 cells are 45.6 px at 800×450** (109 px at 1920×1080), against about 20 px in the centred portrait column. **APPROVED as the desktop/landscape spec (2026-10-25):** every landscape iframe uses this layout, and phones keep the 390×844 portrait column. This replaces the centred-column landscape mock, which is kept only as a reference. Still open: whether 5×5 to 7×7 boards also use it. **Lexend stays the UI font.**
- **Berry tart v2** (`small_size_test_berry_v2.png`: lighter crust #F2C584, lighter berries #B83A5E and a cream halo outside the ink outline). The outer edge contrast on dark patches rises from 1.55 to 3.20:1 on rhubarb, from 1.37 to 2.84 on cocoa and from 1.24 to 2.57 on blueberry. On light patches the ink outline still carries it, and it reads at 24 px and under deuteranopia. **APPROVED by Shahar on 2026-10-25 for option B only.** All B assets are re-rendered with v2: the B mock and its @2x, both landscape mocks, both wide-desktop mocks, and the B rows and frames in the small-size, legibility, cell-state, colour-blind, side-by-side and overview sheets. A and C are frozen, so their mocks and rows keep the old berry. In code, `piece()` maps berry to berry2 for style B, and `_raw=True` gives the old berry.
- **SD art-tooling reuse** (`reuse-sd/REUSE.md`, read from SD @ `a795a1a` with `git show`; provenance in `art/LICENSES.md`):
  - **CVD simulation:** SD uses the same Machado matrices and gives identical output (cross-checked). Ours is kept: it is vectorised, works on whole images, and runs over every pair.
  - **ΔE:** ours is kept. SD uses CIE76, which overstates the gap between light pastels (butter/honey under deutan reads 17 in CIE76 against 10.3 in ΔE2000).
  - **Contrast gate: merged.** SD's role-based PASS/FAIL table is better than our ad-hoc checks. It is now `ttcolor.contrast_table()` plus `gen_palette.py`, adapted from SD's generator to emit the whole Godot `ArtPalette`. The gate caught the star-chip text issue.
  - **Font:** **Lexend stays the default UI font.** Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (copied with its OFL licence to `tools/fonts/`) separates I/l/1 and 0/O better and is smaller (about 30 KB per static weight against Lexend's 175 KB variable file). However, it renders visibly smaller at the same px, our UI has very little alphanumeric text, and Lexend keeps us apart from SD. You can trial it with `TT_UI_FONT=atkinson` (`mocks/mock_B_390x844_atkinson.png`, `tools/font_check_lexend_vs_atkinson.png`). Switch only if Shahar wants the max-legibility option, and then use it at +1–2 px. SD's static files all say "Regular" in their name table, so load them by file.

## B2 revision (Shahar's feedback, 2026-10-25; B is the base, A and C frozen, old B files kept for comparison)
- **Softer board:** each cell is a rounded "cookie" tile. Tiles in the same region are joined by a slightly darker tint of the patch colour, and every region has a soft rim at 0.72 of its own colour, never ink. The parchment lining (#FFF8EC) of the pewter tray shows through between regions. There are no grid lines and no plum borders. Regions stay readable through fill, pattern, the parchment gap and the rim.
- **Empty mold replaces ✕:** a fluted tart-tin outline in ink at 84%, with no fill, halo or shadow, drawn at 62% of the cell. Every cake has a cream halo, a shadow and an opaque fill, so the two read differently even when greyed out. Tool button: "Empty".
- **9 cakes, one per patch:** strawberry→lemon tart, butter→berry tart v2, pistachio→Victoria sponge slice, lavender→apricot tart, honey→cherry Bakewell, blueberry→custard tart, cocoa→rose macaron, rhubarb→scone with jam and cream, vanilla→chocolate tart. All are round or slice-shaped, with no rectangular frosted pastry, no sprinkles, no "pop" and no faces. Code: `tools/ttb2.py`, build script: `tools/build_b2.py`.
- **Files:** `mocks/mock_B2_390x844.png` (+@2x, 8×8), `cakes_closeup_B2.png`, `cell_states_strip_B2.png`, `mocks/desktop_wide_9x9_B2_{1920x1080,800x450}.png` (9×9 cell 45.6 px at 800×450) and `colorblind_check_B2.png`. Check data is in `tools/b2_checks.json`.
- **Checks re-run:** the patch colours are unchanged, so the closest pairs are still ≥10 ΔE2000 under all simulations (deutan butter/honey 10.3, protan lavender/blueberry 10.3, tritan strawberry/honey 10.2).
  - Between each region and the parchment gap, the better of fill or rim reaches ≥2.3:1 (butter and vanilla are lowest; dark patches reach 4.6–5.6).
  - The mold outline against its patch ranges from 3.1:1 (rhubarb) to 6.8:1, which passes the 3:1 non-text bar everywhere.
  - Conflict is still ring + hatch + ! badge.
- **Legibility:**
  - Most readable at 42–46 px cells: berry, lemon, Bakewell, chocolate, apricot, scone.
  - Weakest: the Victoria slice, which is thin at 42 px, and the macaron, which looks like a stack of pale ovals at 24 px.
  - Custard and Bakewell are both pale rounds and are told apart only by the brown centre versus the cherry.
  - Under deuteranopia and protanopia, the rhubarb and cocoa patches both turn mid-grey, so pattern (sprig vs diamond) and rim carry them.
  - Suggestion: thicken the slice by about 15% if it gets lost on phones.
- **B2 cake fixes (2026-10-25), all three resolved at real size.** The previous close-up is kept as `cakes_closeup_B2_v1.png`.
  - **Victoria slice** is 15% thicker and reads as a slice at 42 px.
  - **Macaron** is now a single front-on macaron: a domed shell, one ruffled foot band, a thick cream band and a base shell. Its pink–cream–pink stack reads at 24 px.
  - **Custard tart** has a crimped edge and a golden top with a fine dusting of nutmeg (brown specks, not coloured sprinkles).
  - **Bakewell** has a plain round edge, white icing, one feathered raspberry line and a large cherry.
  - Custard top vs Bakewell icing: ΔE2000 27.7 for normal vision and 27.1 under deuteranopia. They also differ in edge (crimped vs plain) and in specks vs a central cherry.
  - The checks were re-run and are unchanged (patch pairs ≥10.2, gap ≥2.3:1, mold ≥3.1:1).
