# tea-bakery: covers, thumbnails and previews plan (TT Art)
2026-10-25, Asia/Jerusalem. Specs come from `portal/PORTAL_ASSET_SPECS.md` (TT Portal Ops, §1–§6). Its source tags (CG-COV, FF-PB, POKI-THUMB, GD-DES and so on) are reused here. Style: `docs/art/STYLE_GUIDE.md` (B2, locked).
**Hard rules:** no text except the game title. Until the title is chosen every asset is `notext`; the dashed boxes in the concepts only mark where the title will go. No logos: no PKD, no store or app icons. No borders. No sprinkles, no rectangular frosted pastry, no "pop" anything. **No Tunnock's look-alikes:** no red/silver foil-wrapped dome teacakes, and no foil wrapping on any cake (Growth, 2026-10-25). No faces. No coffee, sea or harbour. Avoid colours near #83FFE7. **Covers must match the gated build:** every exported asset is stamped with the build commit.

## 2. Two cover concepts for the Growth A/B test
Renders in `art/covers/`: `concept_A_960x540.jpg`, `concept_B_960x540.jpg`, and their `_square_540.jpg` centre crops. Both are built from the shipped B2 sprites.
### A: "Golden tray" (puzzle-first)
A solved 6×6 board on the golden tray, tilted 6°, with steam rising from three berry cakes. A tiered cake stand and a Victoria slice sit on the right, a floral teapot on the left, and the tea room is blurred behind.
- **Hypothesis:** it reads as "logic puzzle" at a glance (one cake per row and column is visible), which pulls in the Queens/LinkedIn audience.
- **Strengths:** a strong hero, survives every crop, and the 200×120 version is easy (board plus one cake).
- **Risk:** it looks more like a puzzle than a cozy game.
### B: "Recipe book table" (cozy and collection-first)
A board in progress (5×5) on a gingham tea table, three fanned recipe cards (lemon drizzle, Cherry Bakewell, macarons), a copper kettle, teacups on a doily and a Bakewell.
- **Hypothesis:** the collection meta and coziness pull in the women-35+ cozy audience (spec player profile) and raise post-click retention.
- **Risk:** busier. The square and 200×120 versions need a re-stack (board + one card), because the plain centre crop cuts the cards.
- The cards' name ribbons stay blank on covers ("title only" rule).

### Launch cover and challenger (Growth, 2026-10-25)
**Launch with A "Golden tray"** (Growth's lean). **B "Recipe book table" is the challenger.** All CG covers and previews at launch are A only.

### Test design (Growth's input, 2026-10-25)
- **Launch with one cover (A).** Wait about the first 7 days of CG Basic Launch for traffic to settle before testing.
- **Then alternate A / B in 3–4 day blocks, matched on weekdays** (Mon–Thu and Fri–Sun blocks; each arm gets the same number of each, see the schedule below). **Change nothing else in the build or listing during the test:** no build updates, description, tags or title. Each switch swaps only the **3 covers + 2 previews** of that arm.
- **Previews (Growth ruling, 2026-10-25):** each preview set (landscape 1920×1080, portrait 1080×1620) exists in an **A and a B version that differ ONLY in the opening static-cover frame**. The gameplay footage is byte-identical: same capture, same cut, same length; only the first ~0.5 s cover hold and the cover-to-gameplay transition differ. During B blocks, B's covers **and** B's previews go up, so every preview opens on the cover being shown (CG rule).
- **Deciding metric: engaged plays per impression** = cover CTR × the share of clickers who play past ~1 min (from `gameplayStart` + the analytics session timer). **Guardrail:** average playtime must not drop.
- **Approvals:** asking CrazyGames for a split test, or **any listing change**, needs **Shahar's OK and goes through CoS**. **Every block switch is a CG listing change** and goes through Portal Ops. To avoid asking 7 times, Shahar approves the **whole schedule below once, up front**; Portal Ops then executes each switch on its date. Any deviation (a date slip, an extra block, an early stop) goes back to Shahar. TT Art prepares the files only and never uploads.
- **Poki** runs its own thumbnail A/B tests during soft release. Hand Poki both A and B (text-free 1080²) when soft release starts.

### Thumbnail-size sanity check: `art/covers/grid_test.png`
A mock CrazyGames browse grid: 6×3 tiles at 230×130 on a dark background. A sits at row 1 / col 4 and B at row 3 / col 2, among 16 placeholder covers I drew in plain styles (sudoku, blocks, cupcakes, pancakes and so on; no real covers). The grid is shown at actual size and as a "glance" version (¼ scale + blur). Script: `art/tools/grid_test.py`.
**Verdict: A reads faster than B, but both are weaker than the bold placeholders.**
- **A** is one large pastel patchwork square with dots, which reads as "puzzle board". It is the only clear silhouette of either concept at glance size.
- **B** splits attention between the board, three cards and a kettle. At 230×130 the cards turn into pale rectangles, and at glance size B is a beige blur.
- **Both concepts** lose to the saturated placeholders: a pale rosewater/cream background on a dark grid, a hero only about 35–40% of the tile height, and an empty top band (the title placeholder).
- **Fixes before the launch masters (Mon 26):**
  1. Scale A's tray to about 60–65% of the frame height and crop tighter.
  2. Deepen the background: a raspberry-to-rosewater vignette or a darker wall, so the tray pops against CG's dark grid (stay away from #83FFE7).
  3. Raise the cake count and size on the tray so the berry dots read.
  4. Keep the title band compact.

  B gets the same treatment as the challenger (board as hero, one card instead of three). Then re-run the grid test.

### v2 concepts and re-test (2026-10-25, about 10:40): `art/covers/grid_test_v2.png`
`concept_A2_960x540.jpg` / `_square_540.jpg` and `concept_B2_*` (v1 kept). Changes:
- Raspberry-to-pink vignette with a faint cream lattice.
- Tray at about 63% of the frame height, cakes drawn 1.15× cell.
- Compact title band (dashed, no text).
- B2 is board-led with one card (ch5 Cherry Bakewell).
- The squares are **re-stacked 1080² compositions**, not crops: nothing is cut at the edges, and the corners are plain background for Poki's mask.

**Verdict: the v2s are a clear step up from v1, but at glance size they sit mid-pack, not top.**
- Their dark-raspberry edges now frame the tile against the dark grid, and the board reads as a coloured square with dots. They hold their own against the softer placeholders (cookie jar, pancakes, mahjong).
- They still lose to the boldest placeholders (bubbles, blocks, sudoku, donut). Those have one high-contrast shape filling 70–80% of the tile; ours is a pastel board at about 60% inside a mid-value pink.
- **A2 is still ahead of B2.** B2's card and gingham add clutter at 230 px.

**Next levers, without leaving the in-game look:**
1. In the 16:9 cover, scale the tray to about 75–80% of the height and drop the side props (they vanish at 230 px anyway).
2. Darken the vignette centre a step (#E9A9BA rather than #F6C9D3) so the pastel tray separates more.

Then re-run `python3 art/tools/grid_test.py v2`.

### v3 launch cover A3 and re-test (2026-10-25, about 10:45): `art/covers/grid_test_v3.png`
`concept_A3_960x540.jpg` / `_square_540.jpg`:
- Tray at **76% of the frame height**, centred, with no side props.
- Vignette centre darkened to **#E9A9BA**, edges #8E2A4A.
- Steam on three cakes.
- A compact dashed title area above the tray, with no text.

The square is a re-stacked 1080² composition, not a crop, with plain-background corners. The grid compares A3, A2 and B2.
**Verdict: A3 is the best of ours and now level with the single-shape bold placeholders, but still behind the most saturated ones.**
- At glance size it reads as one framed, multicoloured square in a dark-edged pink tile, about as quickly as the sudoku and donut placeholders.
- It still has less punch than the bubbles and blocks tiles, which are saturated colour on near-black. Matching that would need off-model saturation, which the cover-must-match-the-build rule rules out.
- A3 is clearly ahead of A2, and A2 of B2.

**Recommendation:** launch with **A3** as cover A. Rebuild the challenger B on the same recipe (board 76%, one small card, darker centre) before the first B block.
