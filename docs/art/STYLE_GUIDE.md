# tea-bakery: art style guide (CANONICAL)
Owner: TT Art. Status: **LOCKED, style B2** ("B revised", approved by Shahar on 2026-10-25, including berry v2 and the three cake fixes). Source of truth for content: `spec/SPEC.md` v1.0.1 (git 99e0b39) §10 and its mock deltas.
**Which file is canonical:** this file holds the rules. Related docs:
- `art/ART_DIRECTION.md` (gdignored): proposal and decision history (options A/B/C, CVD studies, SD side-by-side). Where they differ, this guide wins.
- `art/HANDOFF_GODOT.md`: integration details per drop.
- `docs/art/ASSET_MANIFEST.md` / `.json`: the file list.
- `docs/art/LICENSES.md`: provenance.

The game is referred to only by its working name **tea-bakery**. **No title, wordmark or studio logo in any art.** The title is pending, so covers use a dashed placeholder.

Regions (index = region id; every region pairs a colour with a **pattern**, so colour is never the only cue):
- 0 strawberry #FA93AC, dots
- 1 butter #FDE87D, stripes
- 2 pistachio #B8DFA6, gingham
- 3 lavender #D5BCF1, lattice
- 4 honey #DDB664, crumb
- 5 blueberry #8E9CC9, shortbread
- 6 cocoa #AE906C, diamond
- 7 rhubarb #CB6F91, sprig
- 8 vanilla #E8E6D9, pinstripe

Every pair stays ≥10 ΔE2000 under normal vision and protan/deutan/tritan simulation (Machado 2009, severity 1.0). Seam = 0.93× fill, rim = 0.72× fill, gap = cream #FFF8EC (≥2.3:1 against every fill and rim). Avoid #83FFE7 (Poki's page background) in covers.

## 3. Typography
- **Lexend** (variable, OFL) for all UI and body text: **≥16 px body/rules (TT-AC-07)**, ≥12 px minimum anywhere, weights 400/500/600, ink on cream or rosewater, cream on raspberry or sage.
- **DM Serif Display** (OFL) for screen titles and recipe names only.
- Text is never baked into images; recipe and décor names are labels.
- Atkinson Hyperlegible Next exists as an option in `art/tools` only; it is not shipped.

**Shape rules:** tarts are round, fluted, lattice or slice-shaped. No rectangular frosted pastry, no sprinkles, no "pop" anything (Kellogg POP-TARTS filings). **No Tunnock's teacake look-alikes:** no red/silver foil-wrapped dome teacakes and no foil wrapping on any cake. This is the same kind of rule as Pop-Tarts (Growth, 2026-10-25). The current assets and both cover concepts were checked: nothing is foil-wrapped, and the only domes are the macaron shell and the scone's cream, both unwrapped and pastel. Adult cozy: no faces, mascots or toy shapes. Inland tea room only: **no coffee, sea or harbour** (TT-AC-38), and no Sudoku Dash look.

## 5. Per-cell and per-screen states
- **Cell** (draw order):
  1. tile
  2. under-layer (solved glow, conflict hatch+ring, hint glow, plate)
  3. cake at 1.0× cell, or mold at 0.70× cell (auto-mold at 0.5 alpha)
  4. badges (!, ✓), the hint target ring and steam
- **Screens** (spec §9): Board (in-progress, conflict, hint, too-small, solved), Solved card, Chapter end (card flip + décor drop), All recipes baked, Home (shelf, streak chip, daily done/not done), Recipe Book (locked/unlocked), Chapter list, Settings (Sound, Music, Auto-molds, Tap order, Reset progress), modals over `SCRIM`.

**Portrait layout notes (ruling 10:19):**
- Rules and body text stay **16 px Lexend, up to 3 lines on phones**. The coach/rules bubble grows from 72 to 88 px for 3 lines.
- The room comes from the vertical gaps (8/8/12 → 4/4/8 on 640 px tall phones), **never** from the font size, the board (≥352 px; 358 px at 390 wide) or the 44 px tap targets.
- Column at 390×844: top bar 56 + 8 + bubble 88 + 8 + tray 380 + 12 + tools 64 = 616 px.
- The wide layout is unchanged (board left at H−40, right panel ≥240 px with the D-02 rules line, Undo/Hint/Reset ≥48 px).

## 6. Cover and store art
See `art/COVERS_PLAN.md` (per `portal/PORTAL_ASSET_SPECS.md`). Covers are built from the shipped sprites, with no text until the title exists (title only, never anything else), no logos, no borders, and no PKD intro.

## 7. Size budget
Art is **3.27 MB** on disk with both scales, or **2.37 MB** shipping only `@2x` plus fonts. That is under 12% of the ≤20 MB first download. Backdrops are lossy WebP (≈20 KB each). Import PNGs as Lossless with mipmaps (mipmaps off for 9-slices).

## 8. Brand
PKD intro + `pkd_boot_splash.png` only; never the Godot default icon or splash. The PKD intro and logo never appear in covers or previews.
