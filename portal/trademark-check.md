# Tea & Tarts: Bakery Logic Puzzle: trademark sanity check (POP-TARTS concern)
Prepared 2026-10-25 (Asia/Jerusalem) by TT Portal Ops; updated 08:35 with Growth's game4-themes.md §d. Research only: nothing filed, submitted or sent.

> **NOT LEGAL CLEARANCE.** This is a desk sanity check by a non-lawyer, using public databases and web results. It is not a clearance search or opinion. EU and international registers were not checked. Before trademark filings, merch or store apps, a trademark attorney should run a full class 9/28/41 search.

**Full title assessed:** "Tea & Tarts: Bakery Logic Puzzle". "Bakery Logic Puzzle" is purely descriptive (genre + theme), so it adds no conflict risk. If anything it reinforces that "tarts" means pastry generically. The distinctive part is "Tea & Tarts", and the analysis below applies to it.
**I'm not a lawyer. This is a desk sanity check, not legal advice or a clearance opinion.** If the title is going to carry real money (merch, store apps, a trademark filing), have a trademark attorney do a full search.

## Bottom line: risk is LOW
- **No identical mark exists.** USPTO has no live or dead record for "TEA AND TARTS" or "TEA & TARTS" (queried the USPTO Trademark Search backend, tmsearch.uspto.gov, on 2026-10-25).
- **The only shared word is "TARTS".** That's a generic, descriptive word for a bakery game, and it already coexists on the register with many third-party TARTS marks: SWEET TARTS, TOOTSIE TARTS, FREEZ-TARTS, SHOCK TARTS, PLANT TARTS, QUEEN OF TARTS and others (table below).
- **Kellogg's documented enforcement targets marks that copy "POP" or the toaster-pastry product.** In each case below, the other party's name copied POP or the product itself:
  - CRUNCH TARTS (for bakery goods): opposition sustained, 1986
  - POPZTARZ: application withdrawn, 2011
  - a restaurant menu item literally called "Pop-Tart": cease-and-desist letter, 2023
- **"Tarts" on its own was accepted.** In the 2023 case the restaurant renamed its item "HG Tarts".
- **Caveat, and why it isn't "very low":** Kellogg is actively expanding POP-TARTS into games. A pending application covers "providing online browser-based video games" (class 41), and another, published for opposition in September 2026, covers card games and jigsaw puzzles (class 28). POP-TARTS is also likely a famous mark, which opens dilution theories. Risk goes up if the art or branding leans on Pop-Tarts trade dress: frosted rectangular toaster pastry with sprinkles, a "pop" pun, or a lookalike logo.
- **Fallback titles:** not needed at this risk level, so none proposed (per the brief).

## 5. Practical guardrails (to keep the risk low)
- Don't use toaster-pastry imagery: no rectangular frosted pastries with sprinkles, no foil-pack visuals.
- No "pop" wordplay in the title, subtitle, level names or store copy.
- Keep the logo font and colour styling away from the Pop-Tarts logo.
- Prefer "Tea & Tarts: Bakery Logic Puzzle" as the full title. The descriptive subtitle reinforces that "tarts" means the pastry generically.
- Before any trademark filing of our own or any merch, run EUIPO/WIPO checks and get attorney clearance.
