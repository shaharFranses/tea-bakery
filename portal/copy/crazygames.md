## Game name
Teacake Lane

## Description
Teacake Lane is a relaxing Queens-style logic puzzle set in a cozy village bakery tea room. Place one cake in every row, every column and every color, but no two cakes may touch, not even at the corners, or they'll stick together in the oven!

Every tray has exactly one solution, and you can always find it with pure logic: no guessing, no timer and no way to fail. Bake your way through 96 levels across 8 chapters, from gentle 5×5 trays to tricky 8×8 ones. Earn up to three stars on each level by solving it without hints. Finish a chapter to collect a new recipe card and a piece of décor for your tea room, with a different cake to bake in every chapter.

Come back each day for the Bake of the Day, a fresh tray that is the same for everyone, and keep your streak going. Stuck? A friendly hint explains the next step in plain words.

A calm brain teaser and daily puzzle for fans of star battle puzzles, sudoku and bakery games.

## Controls
Tap or click a cell to set an empty mold, tap again to place a cake, tap again to clear it. On desktop, right-click sets or clears an empty mold. Undo, Hint and Reset are below the tray on phones and beside it on desktop.
